################################ Extraction from GEE ################################
## Three products are extracted from GEE directly:
SM_ERA5_LAND, VPD_ERA5_LAND, and SM_SMAP_L4

## Two ways to extract: 
1. Extract data from resampled data at a fixed scale (resample: either resample GEE product or input raster to match their spatial projection) 
2. Extract data from raw data at native grid (native grid; extract point-to-point)
The first way is fast but not so accurate due to resampling; in contrast, the second way is very slow but more accurate.

## What I did:
1. For validation using field observations for 2018-2019 (folder: ../work10_Inni_Emission_Water/Figures/FigS_SM_Compare_Evaluate): I used native grid extraction by point.
2. For extracting soil moisture at peatland, non-peatland, and study area for 2003-2025 (folder: ../work10_Inni_Emission_Water/Data/SM_ERA5_LAND (or VPD_ERA5_LAND, and SM_SMAP_L4): I used resample extraction by region.
